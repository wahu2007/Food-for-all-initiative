Our Mission

Food for All Initiative is a Nairobi-based NGO dedicated to fighting food insecurity by providing food to needy families and communities. Our goal is to ensure that everyone has access to safe and nutritious food.

Our Programmes

  • Food distribution to needy families
  • Community farming programmes
  • Nutrition education and awareness
